# Script 3 — Hostile Gatekeeper (Hard)

**Practice Name:** Oakwood Dental Group
**Call Context:** Cold call, first contact, expect gatekeeper

---

> **Prospect (Gatekeeper):** "Oakwood Dental, please hold... [5 seconds] ...okay sorry about that. Who is this?"

[PAUSE — wait for coach hint]

> **Prospect:** "Yeah we're not interested. We get these calls every day."

[PAUSE — coach should suggest a soft pivot, not give up]

> **Prospect:** "Look, she's with a patient, she's not going to take a sales call."

[PAUSE — coach should ask for best time or email]

> **Prospect:** "I don't know, maybe after 4? But honestly she's gonna say no too."

[PAUSE — coach should lock the callback]

> **Prospect:** "Fine. Her name is Dr. Martinez. But don't say I told you to call."

[PAUSE — wait for graceful close]

> **Prospect:** "Mm-hmm. Bye."

**[END CALL — Hostile GK, got callback time + name]**

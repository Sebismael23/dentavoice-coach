# Script 11 — DM "Send Me an Email" (Medium)

**Practice Name:** Horizon Dental
**Call Context:** Cold call, first contact, expect office manager directly

---

> **Prospect (DM):** "Horizon Dental, this is Maria."

[PAUSE — wait for coach hint]

> **Prospect:** "Yeah, can you just send me an email about it?"

[PAUSE — coach should NOT just take the email — try to stay on the call]

> **Prospect:** "I mean, I'm kind of busy right now."

[PAUSE — coach should try a quick hook]

> **Prospect:** "Okay fine. One question. What's the quick version?"

[PAUSE — wait for coach hint — should be a fast pitch or diagnostic]

[PAUSE — coach should explore that]

> **Prospect:** "Probably eight to ten a day? More on Mondays."

[PAUSE — coach should do pain math]

> **Prospect:** "Wow. I never did the math on that. Okay, yeah, I'm interested. Go ahead and send me the details."

[PAUSE — coach should lock email]

> **Prospect:** "maria@horizondental.com. Thanks Seb."

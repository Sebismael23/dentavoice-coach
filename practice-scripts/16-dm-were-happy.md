# Script 16 — DM "We're Happy" (Hard)

**Practice Name:** Pacific Dental Group
**Call Context:** Cold call, first contact, expect office manager directly

---

> **Prospect (DM):** "Pacific Dental Group, this is Stephanie."

[PAUSE — wait for coach hint]

> **Prospect:** "Oh, we're actually really happy with our setup. We've got a great front desk team."

[PAUSE — coach should probe, not accept at face value]

> **Prospect:** "Yeah, we have three people up front. We're pretty well covered."

[PAUSE — coach should find a gap — lunch, after hours, weekends]

> **Prospect:** "Well... I mean after hours obviously nobody's here. But that's just how it is."

[PAUSE — coach should explore that]

> **Prospect:** "I don't know, we get a few calls to voicemail. I check them in the morning."

[PAUSE — coach should ask about callbacks]

> **Prospect:** "Honestly? Probably half of them don't pick up when we call back. They've already gone somewhere else I assume."

[PAUSE — now there's a real pain — coach should quantify]

> **Prospect:** "Hmm. I never thought about it that way. Like actual dollars we're losing."

[PAUSE — coach should move to pitch]

> **Prospect:** "Okay, I'm curious. What would it look like?"

[PAUSE — pitch]

> **Prospect:** "Let me talk to Dr. Chen about it. Can you send me something?"

[PAUSE — coach should lock email + timeline]

> **Prospect:** "stephanie@pacificdentalgroupcom. We have a team meeting Friday."

**[END CALL — "We're happy" → found after-hours gap → interested]**

# Script 17 — DM Impatient (Hard)

**Practice Name:** First Choice Dental
**Call Context:** Cold call, first contact, expect office manager directly

---

> **Prospect (DM):** "First Choice Dental, this is Veronica, make it quick I'm slammed."

[PAUSE — wait for coach hint — needs to be fast and punchy]

> **Prospect:** "Thirty seconds. Go."

[PAUSE — coach should give a fast, high-impact hook]

> **Prospect:** "How many calls? I don't know. A lot. We've got one girl at the front and she's drowning."

[PAUSE — coach should mirror and be quick]

> **Prospect:** "Yeah, probably losing five or six calls over lunch every day. I can see it on the phone system."

[PAUSE — fast pain math]

> **Prospect:** "We know it's a problem. Just haven't had time to deal with it. What's the quick version of what you do?"

[PAUSE — coach should give a fast pitch]

> **Prospect:** "And it just works? I don't have to train anyone or install anything?"

[PAUSE — coach should confirm simplicity]

> **Prospect:** "Fine, send it. veronica@firstchoicedental.com. But if it's a long proposal I'm not reading it."

**[END CALL — Impatient DM, every response must be fast]**

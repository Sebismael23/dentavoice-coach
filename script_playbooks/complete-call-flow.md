__  GATEKEEPER PHASE__

__Stage 1: Opening__

__YOU: __"Hey, quick question — when your front desk is busy with patients, do calls usually go to voicemail or just keep ringing?"

🔑 Calm\. Curious\. Downward inflection\. Like a colleague, not a caller\.

__Stage 2: The Bridge \(after she answers \+ asks who you are\)__

__Formula: ACKNOWLEDGE \(2\-4 words\) → IDENTIFY \(1 sentence\) → REDIRECT \(1 question about THEM\)__

__"How can I help you?"__

__YOU: __"Appreciate that\. I work with practices on phone coverage\. Do those voicemails all get returned same day?"

__"Are you a patient?"__

__YOU: __"No, not a patient\. I work with practices on phone coverage\. Quick question — do those voicemails stack up on busy days?"

__"What is this about?"__

__YOU: __"Fair question\. I help practices catch calls that go to voicemail when busy\. Does that come up for you guys?"

__Flat / Silence__

__YOU: __"Got it\. Most practices tell me some of those never get returned\. Would you say that's true here, or are you on top of it?"

__"Is this a sales call?"__

__YOU: __"I'm not trying to sell anything on this call\. I had a genuine question about your workflow — how do those voicemails get handled when it's busy?"

__Stage 3: Getting the Transfer__

__Goal: Get 3 things — NAME \+ TRANSFER or TIME \+ RELAY SENTENCE__

__She redirects you:__

__THEM: __"You'd need to talk to the office manager about that\."

__YOU: __"Happy to\. What's her name?"

__THEM: __"\[Name\]\."

__YOU: __"Is she around right now?"

__If transferring:__

__YOU: __"Can you tell her it's about phone coverage during busy times? That's all\."

__If not available:__

__YOU: __"When's the best time — morning or afternoon? And her last name so I can ask for her by name?"

__If "just email us":__

__YOU: __"Happy to\. Who should I address it to so it doesn't get lost? I'll follow up in a couple days to make sure it didn't hit spam\."

__Deflections__

__"Send an email" →__

__YOU: __"Happy to\. Just so I send something relevant — are missed calls something that comes up for you guys?"

__"We're good" →__

__YOU: __"Glad to hear it\. Is it because phone coverage isn't an issue, or because you've already solved it?"

__"Call back later" →__

__YOU: __"No problem\. What day and time works best?"

__"Not interested\. Don't call" →__

__YOU: __"Understood\. Thanks for your time\. Have a great day\."

🔑 ONE re\-engage attempt max\. Then honor the exit\. Dental community is small\.

__"We Already Have an Answering Service"__

__YOU: __"That's smart\. When someone calls wanting to book, can they get on the schedule, or do they leave a message?"

90% = just messages\. That's the gap\. If she stays firm:

__YOU: __"Totally respect that\. If anything changes, I'm local\. Can I send a one\-page overview just in case?"

__  DECISION MAKER PHASE__

__⚠️ NEVER SKIP A STAGE\. Each one earns permission for the next\.__

__Stage 4: Warm Open__

__For Office Manager:__

__YOU: __"Hey \[Name\], this is Seb with DentaVoice\. Your team mentioned you handle the front desk systems — appreciate you taking a minute\. I work with practices in Salt Lake on phone coverage\. Would it be unreasonable if I took two minutes?"

__For Dentist:__

__YOU: __"Dr\. \[Name\], this is Seb with DentaVoice\. Appreciate you taking a minute\. I work with practices in Salt Lake on phone coverage\. Would it be unreasonable if I took two minutes?"

__"Sure": __Move to Stage 5\.

__"What do you do?": __15\-sec overview \+ ask permission again\.

__"Only have a minute": __Compressed: one diagnostic Q \+ straight to offer if she engages\.

__"Not interested": __"Is it because coverage isn't a concern, or just bad timing?"

__Stage 5: Diagnostic — Follow Her Thread__

__⚠️ NO pitching\. NO price\. NO product\. You are diagnosing\.__

__THROW the opener:__

__Office Manager:__

__YOU: __"How's your front desk team doing with the workload — enough bandwidth, or is it a stretch?"

__Dentist:__

__YOU: __"How's your schedule looking — booked solid, or gaps you can't explain?"

__LISTEN → FOLLOW:__

__She Mentions\.\.\.__

__Thread__

__Your Next Question__

Staff stress, short\-handed

PEOPLE

"What suffers first — the phones?"

Budget, answering service

MONEY

"Can patients book through them, or just messages?"

Herself doing everything

CHAOS

"How many go to voicemail?" \+ "How does it FEEL?"

Doctor's approval

DOCTOR

"What does Dr\. \[Name\] focus on — costs or growth?"

Schedule gaps, marketing \(dentist\)

REVENUE

"What happens to those calls during busy hours?"

"Not my area"

REDIRECT

"Who handles vendor decisions?"

🔑 Stay in HER world until she runs out of things to say\. Then bridge to yours\.

🔑 Say acknowledgments BETWEEN questions: "That's a lot\." "Makes sense\." "Yeah, that's rough\."

__Keep diagnosing until she's shared real pain \(3\-4 questions\)\. Then move to Stage 6\.__

__⚠️ If she says "we can't afford to hire," do NOT say "what if I solve it for $300\." That's Stage 8\. You're at Stage 5\. Keep diagnosing\.__

__Stage 6: Pain Math__

__Use HER numbers\. Say "conservatively\." Say "even if it's half that\."__

__People / Chaos Thread:__

__YOU: __"Quick math: if calls go to voicemail during busy times, conservatively 30% are new patients\. At $800 each, even losing 2\-3 per week is $80,000\-100,000 per year\. You never see the ones you lost\."

__🤐 PAUSE\. Let the number land\.__

__Money Thread:__

__YOU: __"You're paying \[$X\]/month\. Patients still wait for callbacks\. During that wait, a new patient worth $800\-$1,500 books elsewhere\. You're spending $\[X\]/year on the setup and losing 10\-20x that\."

__Dentist / Revenue Thread:__

__YOU: __"You spend \[$X\]/month on Google Ads to make the phone ring\. Some of those calls go to voicemail during busy hours\. That's like paying for a billboard and covering it during rush hour\."

__YOU: __"If 2\-3 patients per week call, get voicemail, and book elsewhere — what do you think that costs over a year?"

__🤐 LET HIM CALCULATE\. Say nothing\.__

__Stage 7: Label \+ Summary → "That's Right"__

__⚠️ DO NOT SKIP\. This is where trust is built\.__

__Structure: Emotion \+ Situation \+ Consequence__

__YOU: __"So let me make sure I'm hearing you right\. \[Summarize her situation in HER words\. 3\-4 sentences\. Emotion first\.\]"

Examples of what to aim for:

__People: __"You're down a person, phones get missed, patients complained, hiring isn't in the budget\."

__Money: __"You're paying $350/mo but patients still wait\. The gap is costing more than the service\."

__Chaos: __"You're carrying everything alone\. Can't be in two places\. Feel the weight of every missed call\."

__Dentist: __"Invisible gap between patients finding you and getting through\. No report shows what you're missing\."

__🤐 Wait for "That's right" or "That's exactly it\." THEN move to Stage 8\.__

__Stage 8: Grand Slam Offer__

__Thread Frame \(say BEFORE the offer\):__

__People: __"What if \[name\] had a safety net for the phones?"

__Money: __"What if for less than you're paying, patients could book on the call?"

__Chaos: __"What if at end of day, no voicemails to return?"

__Revenue: __"What if every marketing call got answered?"

__Doctor: __"Free pilot\. No decision\. Just: do we want to see the data?"

__THE OFFER:__

__YOU: __"I'll set up a fully configured AI receptionist — free for 14 days\. Answers with your practice name\. Knows hours, insurance\. Books appointments\. Only kicks in when your team can't pick up — safety net, not replacement\. After 2 weeks I show you real data\. $297/mo if you love it\. If not, I unplug it, we shake hands\. No contract\. Would that be worth trying?"

__Stage 9: The Close__

__🤐 SILENCE\. COUNT TO 10\. DO NOT SPEAK\. She is deciding\.__

If 10\+ seconds: "Take your time\. No rush\." Then silence again\.

__Stage 10: Next Steps__

__Yes:__

__YOU: __"Great\. Onboarding email today\. 5 min to fill out\. I walk your team through in 15 min\. What email?"

__Think about it:__

__YOU: __"Of course\. What questions would help you decide? \[If 'just time':\] Recap email tonight\. Check in Thursday?"

__Need doctor approval:__

__YOU: __"Should I send a summary to forward, or a quick 5\-min call with all 3 of us? Pilot is free — no financial decision\. Just: do we want data?"

__No:__

__YOU: __"No problem\. Appreciate your time\. If anything changes, I'm local\. Have a great day\."

Alternative isn't your team — it's voicemail\. AI that books vs\. message nobody returns\.

"What happened?" \[Listen\.\] "That's why pilot is free\. If it happens again, I stop it\."

New patient = $800\-1,500/yr\. One/month = 3x return\. Pilot is free — see results first\.

"Too busy to set up"

Setup is on me — 2 hrs\. Your team: 15 min walkthrough\. Being busy = when calls get missed\.

For pilot: bridge calendar\. Full integration is phase 2 after I understand your workflow\.

"How many clients?"

Building one practice at a time\. Tech handles millions of calls\. I bring local, personal service\.

"We have an answering service"

Can they BOOK or just take messages? \[If messages:\] Same budget, patients book on the spot instead\.

__  EMERGENCY PHRASES__

__YOU: __"That's a fair point\. \[2s pause\] Help me understand\.\.\."

__She goes flat:__

__YOU: __"It seems like this might not be the right time for this conversation\."

__She vents:__

SHUT UP\. Let her finish\. Label emotion\. Summary\. "That's right\."

__Don't know the answer:__

__YOU: __"Great question\. Let me check and include it in the follow\-up\."

__Price at Stage 5:__

__YOU: __"$297/mo, less than 1 patient\. But first — one more question about your situation? \[Return to Stage 5\]"

COUNT TO 10\. If 10\+: "Take your time\. No rush\."

__  STAGE 1: WARM OPEN__

*  Who you are\. Why you're calling\. Permission to continue\.*

__For Office Managers / Staff:__

__YOU: __"Hey \[Name\], this is Seb with DentaVoice\. Your team mentioned you handle the front desk systems — appreciate you taking a minute\. I work with dental practices in the Salt Lake area specifically on phone coverage, and I just had a couple quick questions about how your practice handles busy times\. Would it be unreasonable if I took two minutes of your time?"

__For Dentists:__

__YOU: __"Dr\. \[Name\], this is Seb with DentaVoice\. Appreciate you taking a minute — I know you're between patients\. I work with dental practices in Salt Lake on a specific problem around phone coverage\. Would it be unreasonable if I took two minutes?"

🔑 __*"Would it be unreasonable" is a no\-oriented question\. The natural answer is "No, it's not unreasonable" = yes\. She feels in control because she said no\.*__

__Responses:__

__"Sure, go ahead" \(60%\) → Move to Stage 2\.__

__"What do you do exactly?" \(20%\) → 15\-second overview, then ask permission again\.__

__"I only have a minute" \(15%\) → Compressed version: one diagnostic question \+ straight to offer if she engages\.__

__"Not interested" \(5%\) → One re\-engage: "Is it because phone coverage isn't a concern, or just bad timing?" Then honor the exit\.__

__➡ PRACTICE WARM OPEN: 10 REPS \(5 as office manager, 5 as dentist\)__

__  STAGE 2: THE DIAGNOSTIC \(Thread\-Following\)__

*  Ask questions about what SHE deals with every day\. Not what the front desk sees\.*

For office managers / staff:

__YOU: __"How's your front desk team doing with the workload right now — enough bandwidth, or is it a stretch?"

For dentists:

__YOU: __"How's your schedule looking these days — booked solid, or do you see gaps you can't quite explain?"

*⚠️ If she says "we can't afford to hire someone," do NOT say "what if I solve it for $300?" That's Stage 5\. You're in Stage 2\. Keep diagnosing: "Yeah, $20\-30K is a lot\. So when calls go unanswered during the rush, do you have any sense of how many might be new patients?"*

## __PEOPLE Thread — Follow\-Up Questions__

__Q1: What breaks first?__

__YOU: __"That's a lot on one person\. When it gets really hectic, what usually gives first — is it the phones?"

__Q2: Patient complaints?__

__YOU: __"Have any patients mentioned having trouble getting through on the phone?"

__Q3: The invisible loss__

__YOU: __"She's the one who came in\. How many do you think just call somewhere else and you never hear from them?"

__Q4: Solution attempts__

__YOU: __"Has anyone ever looked into any kind of phone coverage solution, or talked about hiring just for phones?"

After 3\-4 questions, you have enough pain\. Move to Stage 3\.

__➡ PRACTICE ALL 4 FOLLOW\-UPS: 5 REPS EACH__

## __MONEY Thread — Follow\-Up Questions__

__Q1: Current spend__

__YOU: __"What are you paying for the answering service, if you don't mind?"

__Q2: Gap question__

__YOU: __"When someone calls the service wanting to book, can they actually get on the schedule, or do they leave a message?"

__Q3: Callback delay__

__YOU: __"How quickly do those messages get to your team?"

__Q4: The wait reframe__

__YOU: __"So if a new patient calls at 2 PM, talks to the service, leaves a message, and hears back from your team the next morning — that's an 18\-hour wait for someone who's probably looking at 3 or 4 practices on Google\."

After the wait reframe, move to Stage 3 \(pain math with her actual cost numbers\)\.

__➡ PRACTICE ALL 4: 5 REPS EACH__

## __CHAOS Thread — Follow\-Up Questions__

__Q1: Volume \(she knows this\)__

__YOU: __"When you're busy with patients at the counter, how many calls go to voicemail?"

__Q2: The emotional question__

__YOU: __"How does it feel when the phone's ringing and you've got someone right in front of you and you can't get to both?"

__Q3: New patients__

__YOU: __"Do you know which of those missed calls are new patients?"

__Q4: Callback reality__

__YOU: __"Those new patient voicemails — how quickly are you getting back to them?"

After the vent \+ callback question, move to Stage 3\.

__➡ PRACTICE ALL 4: 5 REPS\. Focus on silence after Q2\.__

## __REVENUE Thread \(Dentist\) — Follow\-Up Questions__

__Q2: The blind spot__

__➡ PRACTICE ALL 3: 5 REPS EACH__

__  STAGE 3: PAIN MATH__

*  Translate the problem into dollars using HER answers\.*

🔑 __*She told you the situation at Stage 2\. Now you help her see the COST of that situation\. Use HER numbers\. Say "conservatively\." Say "even if it's half that\." Let HER feel the weight\.*__

__For People/Chaos Threads \(staff\-focused\):__

__YOU: __"Quick math with what you told me\. If calls are going to voicemail during busy times, conservatively 30% of those are probably new patients\. At $800\-1,000 per new patient per year, even losing 2\-3 per week adds up to $80,000\-$150,000 per year\. And the hard part is — you never see the ones you lost\."

__*🤐 PAUSE\. Let the number land\.*__

__For Money Thread \(compare to current spend\):__

__YOU: __"So you're paying \[$350\] a month and patients still have to wait for a callback\. During that wait, a new patient worth $800\-$1,500 might book elsewhere\. If even 2\-3 per week slip through, that's $80,000\+ a year walking out the door\. You're spending $4,200 a year on the current setup and potentially losing 10\-20 times that\."

__*🤐 PAUSE\.*__

__For Dentist/Revenue Thread \(marketing waste frame\):__

__YOU: __"You're spending \[$3,000\] a month to make the phone ring\. Some of those calls go to voicemail during busy hours\. That's like paying for a billboard and covering it during rush hour\."

__YOU: __"If even 2\-3 patients per week call, get voicemail, and book elsewhere — what do you think that costs over a year?"

__*🤐 LET HIM DO THE MATH\. Say nothing\. He'll get to $100K\+\.*__

__For Doctor Thread \(abbreviated\):__

__YOU: __"Most practices find they're losing $50,000\-100,000\+ per year in missed new patient calls during busy hours\. The tricky part is there's no report that shows it — it's completely invisible\."

After the math lands \(she pauses, says "wow" or "I never thought about that"\), move to Stage 4\.

__➡ PRACTICE PAIN MATH: 3 REPS FOR EACH THREAD \(12 total\)__

__  STAGE 4: LABEL \+ SUMMARY__

*  Prove you heard her\. Aim for "that's right\."*

🔑 __*"That's right" is the turning point\. Before it, she's evaluating you\. After it, she's open\. Take HER words from Stage 2, her EMOTION, and her SITUATION, and reflect them back\.*__

*⚠️ Do NOT skip this stage\. Do NOT rush to the offer after the pain math\. The summary is what converts "interesting data" into "I trust this person to solve my problem\."*

__People Thread Summary:__

__YOU: __"So let me make sure I'm hearing you right\. You're down a person, \[name\] is handling everything at the front desk alone, phones get missed when it's busy, patients have complained about not getting through, and you know some are probably calling somewhere else\. But hiring someone just for phones isn't in the budget\."

__Money Thread Summary:__

__YOU: __"So you've already invested in solving this — you're paying \[$350\] a month\. But the service takes messages and patients still wait for a callback\. During that wait, new patients are probably booking elsewhere\. You're paying for coverage that doesn't actually close the gap\."

__Chaos Thread Summary:__

__YOU: __"It sounds like you're carrying everything on your own\. Check\-ins, insurance, phones, billing — and when it gets busy, you can't be in two places at once\. Calls go to voicemail, some are new patients, and you feel the weight of every one you can't get to\."

__Dentist/Revenue Thread Summary:__

__YOU: __"It sounds like the practice is strong clinically, but there's an invisible gap between patients finding you and getting through\. You're investing in marketing, they're calling, but some of those calls land in voicemail during busy hours and never convert\. And there's no report that shows you what you're missing\."

__Doctor Thread Summary \(abbreviated\):__

__YOU: __"It sounds like the phones get busy, calls are being missed, and those missed calls might be connected to the schedule gaps Dr\. \[Name\] has been asking about\."

After each summary, wait\. If she says "that's right" or "that's exactly it" or "yeah" with conviction → move to Stage 5\.

__➡ PRACTICE ALL 5 SUMMARIES: 3 REPS EACH__

__  STAGE 5: THE GRAND SLAM OFFER__

*  Free 14\-day pilot\. Maximum value\. Zero risk\.*

🔑 __*The pilot replaces the old audit\. It IS the audit — but better, because it captures real patient calls, not simulated tests\. After 14 days she has real data proving the value\.*__

__The Offer \(same for everyone\):__

__YOU: __"Here's what I'd like to do\. I'll set up a fully configured AI receptionist for your practice — free for 14 days\. It answers with your practice name, knows your hours, your services, your insurance info, and books appointments directly\. It only kicks in when your team can't pick up — so it's a safety net, not a replacement\. After two weeks, I send you a report showing exactly how many calls it caught, how many were new patients, and how many booked\. Real data, not guesses\. If you love it, $297 a month\. If you don't, I unplug it and we shake hands\. No contract\. No setup fee\. Would that be worth trying?"

🔑 __*"Safety net, not a replacement" addresses the fear of replacing staff\. "Real data, not guesses" is the value promise\. "I unplug it and we shake hands" makes cancellation visceral and easy\. "Would that be worth trying" = lowest commitment language possible\.*__

__Thread\-Specific Framing \(add BEFORE the offer\):__

__People Thread — frame as staff relief:__

__YOU: __"What if \[name\] had a safety net for the phones? So when she's with a patient and the phone rings, it just gets handled — and she doesn't have to feel guilty about it\."

__Money Thread — frame as cost swap:__

__YOU: __"What if for less than what you're paying now, patients could actually book on the call instead of leaving a message?"

__Chaos Thread — frame as personal relief:__

__YOU: __"What if at the end of the day, there were no voicemails to return — because every call got handled while you were busy?"

__Revenue Thread \(Dentist\) — frame as marketing ROI:__

__YOU: __"What if every call your marketing generates actually got answered — and the patients who couldn't reach your team still booked?"

__Doctor Thread — frame for internal sell:__

__YOU: __"Here's what I'd suggest\. The pilot is free and there's no commitment\. So what you'd be sharing with Dr\. \[Name\] is: 'this company wants to test our phone coverage for 14 days, show us the real numbers, and it costs us nothing\.' That's a pretty easy yes\."

After the thread\-specific frame, deliver the full Grand Slam offer, then STOP TALKING\.

__➡ PRACTICE: Thread frame \+ full offer = 7 REPS \(one per thread \+ 2 extra\)__

__"Let's try it\."__

__THEM: __"Okay, let's do it\."

__YOU: __"\[Move to Stage 7: Next Steps\. Do NOT over\-celebrate\.\]"

__"How much after the trial?"__

__YOU: __"$297 a month, everything included\. 24/7 coverage, unlimited calls, appointment booking, and I personally monitor your account\. That's less than one new patient is worth\. If it catches even one per month, it pays for itself multiple times\."

__THEM: __"Let me think about it\."

__"We tried this before\."__

__YOU: __"What happened?"

__YOU: __"That makes sense\. That's exactly why the pilot is free\. If it's happening again, you tell me and I stop it\. Would it be worth seeing how different it is?"

__"Too expensive\."__

__THEM: __"$297 seems like a lot\."

__YOU: __"What do you think a new patient is worth in the first year? If the AI catches even one per month that would've gone to voicemail, that's a 3\-to\-1 return minimum\. And during the pilot, it's free — so you'd see exactly what it captures before spending a dollar\."

__"No\."__

__THEM: __"I don't think it's for us\."

__YOU: __"No problem at all\. I appreciate your time\. If anything changes, I'm local and always happy to help\. Have a great day\."

__➡ PRACTICE ALL 8 RESPONSES: 3 REPS EACH__

__  STAGE 6: THE CLOSE__

*  Ask\. Shut up\. The silence does the work\.*

The close is the last line of the Grand Slam offer: "Would that be worth trying?" followed by absolute silence\.

🔑 __*Count to 10 in your head\. Do NOT fill the void\. Do NOT add "and also we include\.\.\." Do NOT rephrase the question\. She is deciding\. Every word you say during her decision INTERRUPTS her internal yes\.*__

If silence hits 10\+ seconds and she hasn't spoken:

__YOU: __"Take your time\. No rush\."

Then silence again\. Let her break it\.

__➡ SILENCE DRILL: Say the offer, ask the question, sit for 10 seconds\. 5 REPS\.__

__After "Need to talk to doctor":__

__YOU: __"I'll send a one\-page summary tonight that you can forward\. Can I check in \[specific day\] if I haven't heard back?"

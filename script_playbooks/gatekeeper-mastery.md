## __The Three Moments That Kill Your Calls__

Every call you’ve lost died at one of these three moments\. Fix these three things and your conversion rate doubles overnight\.

### __Kill Moment \#1: The Test__

When the receptionist asks “Who are you?” or “What are you selling?” or “Why are you asking?” — you freeze, get vague, or dodge the question\. They categorize you as evasive\. Call dies\.

__What you’ve been saying: __*“I’m not currently selling anything right now\. I work with dental practices in the area, and I was calling to get a couple information\.”*

__⚠️ COACH: __*This is evasive\. She asked a direct question\. You dodged it\. Trust drops to zero\.*

__What you should say: __*“This is Seb with DentaVoice\. We help practices capture calls when the front desk is busy\.”*

Three pieces: Name\. Company\. One\-sentence purpose\. Every single time\. No exceptions\.

### __Kill Moment \#2: The Missing Gap Question__

After they tell you calls go to voicemail, you jump straight to asking for the decision\-maker\. You skip the question that creates DOUBT about their current system and gives you leverage\.

__The gap question you keep skipping: __*“When those voicemails do get returned, does the patient usually pick up, or have some already booked somewhere else by then?”*

This question plants a seed: even if they return calls, some patients are already gone\. It creates urgency without being pushy\.

### __Kill Moment \#3: The Empty Exit__

When they say “not interested,” you say “of course, have a great one” and hang up with nothing\. No email\. No website\. No callback time\. No name\. Nothing\.

__Your graceful exit \(say it every time\): __*“Totally fair\. If phone coverage ever becomes a concern, we’re at dentavoice\.co\. Have a great day\.”*

Get “dentavoice\.co” out in the FIRST BREATH after she says no\. If she hangs up fast, she still heard your website\.

 

# __PART 2: THE COMPLETE GATEKEEPER FRAMEWORK__

Every cold call to a dental practice follows a predictable structure\. The receptionist will do one of 6 things\. If you can identify WHICH thing she’s doing within 3 seconds, you always know what to do next\.

## __The 6 Intents \(Memorize These\)__

__Intent__

__What She’s Doing__

__What It Sounds Like__

__Your Move__

GUARDING

Walls up\. Short answers\. Protecting from sales pitch\.

"Mmhmm\." "Yeah\." "Voicemail\." Flat tone\.

LABEL the guard, then ask a WHAT question\.

TESTING

Checking if you’re legit before she engages\.

"Who is this?" "What company?" "Is this a sales call?"

Be DIRECT\. Name \+ company \+ one sentence\. Then pivot to a question\.

DEFLECTING

Trying to end the call without confrontation\.

"Send an email\." "We’re good\." "Not interested\."

AGREE, then ONE calibrated question, then honor the exit\.

VENTING

Sharing real frustration about the problem you solve\.

Long answers\. Stories\. Emotion\. "Oh my gosh, you have NO idea\.\.\."

SHUT UP\. Let her talk\. Label the emotion\. Aim for "That’s right\."

ENGAGING

Genuinely curious\. Asking real questions\.

"How does that work?" "What does it cost?"

Answer briefly \(2\-3 sentences\)\. Then question back\.

DECIDING

Processing\. Weighing options internally\.

Long pauses\. "Hmm\." "Let me think\."

STAY SILENT\. Count to 10\. Let her break the silence\.

## __The Universal Recovery \(When You’re Lost\)__

No matter what happens on a call, these three steps get you back on track:

__STEP 1: PAUSE\. __Stop talking\. Breathe\. Count to 2\. Silence makes you sound confident\.

__STEP 2: LABEL\. __“It sounds like\.\.\.” or “It seems like\.\.\.” This buys you time AND makes them feel heard\.

__STEP 3: CALIBRATED QUESTION\. __Ask a “What” or “How” question\. These can’t be answered with one word\.

When in absolute doubt, say these 8 words: “That’s a fair point\. Help me understand\.\.\.” This works in literally any situation\.

 

# __PART 3: THE COMPLETE CALL FLOW \(STAGES 1–4\)__

This is the GPS for every cold call\. The goal is NOT to pitch the doctor on this call\. The goal is to get ONE of these outcomes: \(1\) Transfer to the decision\-maker, \(2\) Decision\-maker’s name \+ callback time, \(3\) Email address with a warm handoff, \(4\) Receptionist recruited as internal champion\.

## __STAGE 1: OPENING / PATTERN INTERRUPT \(0–10 seconds\)__

Goal: Break their mental pattern of “this is a sales call, hang up\.” Sound human\. Earn 10 more seconds\.

__THEM: __"Thank you for calling \[Practice Name\], this is \[Name\], how can I help you?"

__YOU: __"Hey \[Name\] — quick one\."

__🎤 TONE: __*Casual\. Like calling a colleague\. Downward inflection on “quick one\.” Smile while you say it\. Pause 1 FULL second after\.*

__🎯 TECHNIQUE: __*Voss pattern interrupt\. They expect “Hi this is X from Y company\.” You sound like someone who already knows them\.*

__➡ SAY THIS 10 TIMES OUT LOUD BEFORE EVERY CALL SESSION\.__

__Then immediately move to the diagnostic question:__

__YOU: __"When your front desk is already busy helping patients, do incoming calls go to voicemail or keep ringing?"

__🎤 TONE: __*Genuine curiosity\. Slow\. Downward inflection\. You actually want to know the answer\.*

__🎯 TECHNIQUE: __*Calibrated question\. Both answers reveal pain\. You’re diagnosing, not selling\. This is what separates you from every other cold caller\.*

__What if they don’t give their name?__

Some receptionists answer with just the practice name: “\[Practice Name\], how can I help you?” In this case, skip the name and go straight to “Hey — quick one\.”

## __STAGE 2: HANDLE THE RESPONSE \(10–45 seconds\)__

After your diagnostic question, she’ll respond\. Her response tells you which intent she’s in\. Read it, then execute the right move\.

### __Response Path A: She Answers the Question__

__THEM: __"They go to voicemail\."

__YOU: __"Voicemail?"

__🎤 TONE: __*Mirror\. Upward inflection\. Curious, not surprised\. Then SILENCE for 3 seconds\.*

If she elaborates — great\. Label what she says:

__THEM: __"Yeah, when we’re busy we try to call back but sometimes we don’t get to it\."

__YOU: __"It sounds like you’re juggling a lot up there\."

__🎤 TONE: __*Warm\. Empathetic\. Late\-night FM DJ voice\. Let it land\.*

If the mirror gets only “Mmhmm” \(GUARDING\), don’t mirror again\. Label instead:

__YOU: __"That’s helpful — appreciate it\. It sounds like you get a lot of these kinds of calls\."

__CRITICAL: Now ask the GAP QUESTION before asking for the decision\-maker:__

__YOU: __"And when those voicemails do get returned — does the patient usually pick up, or have some already booked somewhere else by then?"

__🎯 TECHNIQUE: __*This plants doubt\. Even if they return calls same day, some patients have already called another practice\. This is your leverage\.*

__Alternative gap questions:__

__YOU: __"Does your current system book appointments directly, or do patients have to call back to confirm?"

__YOU: __"What happens to the calls that come in during your busiest hour — like 9 to 10 AM?"

### __Response Path B: She Tests You__

She asks “Who is this?” or “Why are you asking?” or “What are you selling?”

__This is TESTING intent\. She wants to categorize you\. Answer DIRECTLY\. Do NOT dodge\.__

__THEM: __"Why are you asking?"

__YOU: __"Fair question\. This is Seb with DentaVoice\. We help practices capture calls when the front desk is busy\. I was just curious how you guys handle it when everyone’s tied up with patients\."

__🎤 TONE: __*Direct\. Calm\. Zero pressure\. One breath\.*

__THEM: __"What company are you with? What are you selling?"

__YOU: __"This is Seb with DentaVoice\. We help practices capture calls when the front desk is busy\. Quick question — when everyone’s with patients, what happens to those incoming calls?"

__🎯 TECHNIQUE: __*Answer \+ redirect\. Name, company, purpose, then pivot back to diagnostic question\. All one breath\.*

__THEM: __"Is this a sales call?"

__YOU: __"I’m not trying to sell you anything on this call\. I help practices with phone coverage and I just had a quick question about how yours handles busy times\."

__⚠️ COACH: __*NEVER say “No, this isn’t a sales call\.” That’s dishonest and she’ll feel manipulated\. “Not on this call” is truthful — you’re diagnosing, not closing\.*

### __Response Path C: She Deflects__

She tries to end the call: “We’re not interested,” “Send an email,” “We’re good\.”

__“We’re not interested”:__

__YOU: __"Totally understandable\. It sounds like you get a lot of these calls\."

If she responds, you’re back in conversation\. If she doubles down — graceful exit\.

__“Send an email”:__

__YOU: __"Absolutely, I’ll send that over\. Quick question — when calls come in and you’re all with patients, what happens to those?"

If she engages, you’re in\. If she repeats “just email”:

__YOU: __"Got it\. Who should I address it to, just so it doesn’t get buried?"

__🎯 TECHNIQUE: __*Getting a specific name means your email goes to a person, not an inbox black hole\.*

__“We’re happy with how things are”:__

__YOU: __"It sounds like you’ve got a pretty solid system\. Quick question though — when you’re all with patients at the same time, does anything ever slip through?"

__🎯 TECHNIQUE: __*Label that AGREES with her \(confuses the guard — salespeople don’t agree\), then calibrated question that exposes gaps\.*

 

## __STAGE 3: GET TO THE DECISION\-MAKER \(45–90 seconds\)__

__Once you’ve established rapport AND asked the gap question, NOW you ask for the transfer\. Not before\.__

__YOU: __"That makes sense\. Hey, who usually handles decisions about phone coverage — is that Dr\. \[Name\] or is there an office manager?"

__🎤 TONE: __*Casual\. “Hey” keeps it conversational\. You’re asking for direction, not permission\.*

__If the doctor is busy:__

__THEM: __"Dr\. Smith is with a patient right now\."

__YOU: __"With a patient right now?"

__🎤 TONE: __*Mirror\. Upward inflection\.*

__THEM: __"Yeah, booked solid until 3\."

__YOU: __"Got it\. Would it be unreasonable for me to try back around 3:15?"

__🎯 TECHNIQUE: __*Voss no\-oriented question\. “Unreasonable” invites “no” = “No, that’s fine\.”*

__THEM: __"No, that should be okay\."

__YOU: __"Perfect\. And just so I don’t catch you at a bad time again — is there a better number or should I call this line?"

__If she blocks hard \(“We don’t take sales calls”\):__

__YOU: __"Totally fair\."

__🎤 TONE: __*PAUSE 2 full seconds\. No pushback energy\. Warm\.*

__YOU: __"It sounds like you get a lot of these\."

__THEM: __"Yeah, like five a day\."

__YOU: __"That must be exhausting on top of everything else\. Look, I’m not going to waste your time\. Can I just send a one\-page info sheet to the office email? If it’s not relevant, delete it\."

__🎯 TECHNIQUE: __*You didn’t fight\. You validated her frustration\. You asked for the lowest possible commitment\. She can say yes without feeling like she agreed to anything\.*
